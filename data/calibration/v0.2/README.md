# Glance Hair Perception Calibration Dataset v0.2

## Overview
- **Dataset Version**: `calibration-v0.2`
- **Total Samples**: 24 images + contact sheet
- **Generator Model**: FLUX.2-klein-4B
- **Status**: Historical / Immutable

## Evolution from v0.1
Calibration v0.2 was generated with modified prompt templates and enhanced anatomical structural constraints:
- Reinforced shoulder landmark visibility and torso proportions.
- Clarified intermediate landmarks (`BOB` vs `SHORT` vs `SHOULDER`).
- Improved lighting neutrality and hair silhouette definition to reduce ambiguity in automated and human visual evaluation.
